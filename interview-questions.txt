CISCO NETCONNECT PUP - MANILA
INTERVIEW QUESTIONS BY DEPARTMENT AND ROLE
==========================================

--------------------------------------------
TECHNOLOGY DEPARTMENT
--------------------------------------------

Enterprise Networking Cadet
1. What interests you about learning how computer networks work, and what do you hope to gain from the Enterprise Networking track?
2. If you could set up or troubleshoot a network for a school or small office, what would you want to learn first?

Cybersecurity Operations Cadet
1. What interests you about cybersecurity and protecting computer systems, and what do you hope to gain from the Cybersecurity Operations track?
2. If you could learn one skill to keep networks safe from threats, what would it be and why?

Developer Network Cadet
1. What interests you about programming and automation, and what do you hope to gain from the Developer Network track?
2. If you could build a tool that automates a repetitive task, what kind of tool would you want to create?

RELATIONS DEPARTMENT - External
--------------------------------------------

Community Partnership Lead
1. How would you identify and establish partnerships with other student organizations and academic communities to strengthen CNCP's presence?

Community Partnership Co-Lead
1. How would you support the Community Partnership Lead in managing external relationships and coordinating partnership activities?
2. How would you handle a situation where a potential partner organization is unresponsive to outreach?

Sponsors Lead

Sponsors Co-Lead
